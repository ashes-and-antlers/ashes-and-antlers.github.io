Entry 01 One galaxy, two powers

Both powers are born into the same finite galaxy — every sector, system, and planet drawn from a single seed. They develop the same worlds, mine the same resources, and feel the same pressures: growth, scarcity, distance, war.

No outcome is scripted. Both powers run under identical rules, and every result traces back to a cause you can find in the galaxy's own record.

Entry 02 The two powers

The Hearth Confederacy

Fortress-world builders preserving old civic institutions, hearth-reactors, and archive networks. Strong infrastructure, energy stability, and a reliable economy — but what they build, they must defend.

The Iron Swarm

Mobile caste fleets that strip, repurpose, and rapidly establish nests on frontier worlds. Fast scouting, raiding, and colonization pressure — but concentrated losses cut deep.

You command one power. The other answers in kind.

Entry 03 The rules of the archive

  1. 01

    A deterministic core

    Same seed, same commands, same world — every time. Reproducibility is tested, not hoped for.

  2. 02

    Simulation first

    Every visible outcome has a causal chain in simulation state. Nothing is faked to make a story.

  3. 03

    Readable complexity

    Alerts, inspectors, and overlays answer three questions: what changed, why, and what you can do.

  4. 04

    Timing decides wars

    Fleets travel in ticks, not seconds. Supply, staging, and intelligence decide wars — planning beats click speed.
